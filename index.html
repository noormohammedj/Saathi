The ask

From occasional visitor to committed devotee

ISKCON Coimbatore asked for a platform that helps visitors return regularly, adopt the bhakti process and offer their skills in seva, with credits they can redeem. Here is how each part of that ask is answered.

Visitors come and go – how do we make them visit regularly?

Geo + QR temple check-in, daily streaks with a 30-day bonus, and reminders before aratis and programmes.

Help them adopt the bhakti process

A 108-bead japa counter, Gita chapter tracker, lecture hearing log and a five-week sadhana heatmap.

Let them offer services based on their skills

Skill profiles, festival roles tagged by skill, and a 'needs your skills' feed for every devotee.

Visitors can club together for meaningful seva

Swipe-to-connect matching, seva teams with group chat, and circles as micro-communities.

Credits for visits, programmes, Gita, lectures and seva

An auditable credit ledger with configurable rules and daily caps, verified by geo, QR or a coordinator.

Redeem for gifts, prasadam, priority passes, archana

A rewards catalogue that issues a code to show at the temple counter.

Functionality

What Saathi does

01
Skill-based Saathi matching

A seva-match score weighs complementary skills for open festival roles, shared circles, availability, language and sadhana streak.

02
Swipe, connect, invite

Right to connect, left for later, up to send a seva invite. Mutual connections get 24 hours to send the first Hare Krishna.

03
Seva teams

Form a team for any event in one tap; a group chat opens and coordinators are notified.

04
Private in-app chat

Devotees never see each other's phone numbers or emails. Messages are moderated by the temple.

05
Festival & programme roles

Every event lists roles by skill with live staffing, credits per role and one-tap volunteering.

06
Daily sadhana tracker

Japa counter (108 beads × 16 rounds), Gita chapters, lectures and temple visits build streaks.

07
Verified temple check-in

Location within 150 m of the mandir plus a rotating QR at the entrance stops credits from home.

08
Credits & rewards

Earn for visits, programmes, japa, Gita, lectures and verified seva. Redeem for prasadam, archana and passes.

09
Donations & sevas

Annadaan, Kovai Chandra, Gau Seva, Vidya Daan and Vaishnav Bhojan with the temple's real tiers and 80G receipts.

10
Circles

Micro-communities by skill or practice – Japa Sunrise Club, Kirtan Mandali, Gita Circle, Seva Tech Guild.

11
No-skill seva paths

Chanting, serving, welcoming and kitchen roles at every event so every visitor has a place.

12
Temple admin console

Member directory with admin-only contacts, engagement funnel, festival staffing, redemptions and credit rules.

For devotees

  • Find people who complete their skills and serve together, not alone
  • A simple daily practice with visible progress and streaks
  • Meaningful recognition: prasadam, archana and festival priority
  • Privacy by design – contact details stay with the temple

For the temple

  • Turns one-time visitors into weekly regulars, measured on a funnel
  • Festivals staffed by skill weeks in advance, not on the day
  • A living directory of skills across the Coimbatore community
  • More regular, transparent donations with automatic 80G receipts

Honest limits

What the prototype doesn't do yet

  • Uses sample data on the device – there is no live server yet, so devotees on different phones don't see each other.
  • Chat replies are simulated to show the experience.
  • Temple check-in simulates location; production uses GPS + a rotating QR code at the entrance.
  • No payments are taken; donation tiers are real but the UPI step is a placeholder.
  • Admin mode is a switch for the demo; production grants the role on the server.
  • Contact details are masked in this public demo.
  • Festival dates and role counts are samples to be confirmed with the temple calendar.
  • The web version runs the same code as the phone app, but haptics and some native polish only appear on a real device.

Growth path

Where Saathi goes next

1
Coimbatore first

Pilot with 50 core volunteers, then the whole Sunday feast community. Prove the visitor-to-regular funnel.

2
Every programme

Bhakti Vriksha groups, youth programmes (Spark, Vrinda, Folk) and Gita Life for corporates run their circles in Saathi.

3
Languages

Tamil and Hindi so every devotee, including elders, can take part.

4
Smarter matching

AI suggestions for teams and mentors – pairing new devotees with experienced Saathis.

5
Other ISKCON centres

The platform is multi-temple from day one: each centre adds its own sevas, rewards and credit rules.

6
Sustainable

Running costs stay low and can be covered by a tiny share of the extra donations it brings in.
